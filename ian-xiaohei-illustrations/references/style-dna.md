# Style DNA

## In One Sentence

Pure white, minimal, hand-drawn, spacious, restrained, strange, product-sketch-like, with handwritten English and a clear structure that does not feel like an instruction manual.

It should look like an explanatory sketch casually drawn on white paper by someone who has spent years working with AI, products, design, and developer tools.

## Requirements

- 16:9 landscape inline illustration.
- Pure white background: no beige, warm gray, paper texture, gradients, shadows, noise, or vintage-paper feel.
- Primarily black hand-drawn line art: thin, slightly wobbly, not mechanical, not vector-like, and without heavy outlines.
- Plenty of white space: the subject occupies about 40%-60% of the canvas, with at least 35% empty space and preferably one large quiet area.
- Sparse handwritten English annotations: at most 5-8 labels, ideally 1-4 words each.
- One core action, structure, state, or metaphor per image.
- Express the structure naturally; do not write the structure type on the image.

## Colors

- Black: Main line art, character, borders, structure, primary text, and main objects.
- Red: Emphasis, problems, emotional points, key warnings, and results.
- Orange: Main flow, paths, arrows, automation direction, and movement from A to B.
- Blue: Secondary notes, mental state, system state, second-layer explanations, and AI/assistant/automation cues.

Blue is not required in every image. Keep colors restrained; less is better than more.

## Never Do This

- No commercial illustration.
- No PPT infographic.
- No formal flowchart.
- No course slide.
- No cute cartoon poster.
- No children's illustration.
- No complex architecture diagram.
- No polished flat illustration.
- No futuristic UI.
- No real app screenshot.
- No complex backgrounds, gradients, shadows, or textures.
- Do not explain every node.
- Do not write type titles such as "Workflow / Flowchart," "System Architecture," "Common Pitfalls," or "Roadmap" in the top-left corner.

## Aesthetic Direction

Be strange, creative, interesting, simple, clean, and imaginative.

Do not be cute, childish, complex, or rigid.
